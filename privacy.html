Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction

WhatsApp AI Panel ("we", "our", "us") provides an AI-powered customer support tool for businesses using WhatsApp. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Business Information: Business name, email, phone number, and WhatsApp Business Account details.
  • Customer Messages: Messages exchanged between businesses and their customers via WhatsApp.
  • Usage Data: How you use our platform, including login times, features used, and API calls.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our services
  • To process and respond to messages via AI
  • To send transactional and service-related notifications
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Meta Platforms: To deliver WhatsApp messages
  • AI Providers: Google (Gemini), Groq for message processing
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption, HTTPS, and access controls. Customer messages are encrypted in transit and at rest.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time.

7. Your Rights (DPDP Act, 2023 - India)

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure (deletion)
  • Right to withdraw consent
  • Right to grievance redressal

8. Data Deletion

To delete your data, see our Data Deletion Instructions.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be notified via email.

11. Contact Us
