FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need coding knowledge?

Not at all. Our setup wizard takes 10 minutes. Just fill in your business details and AI is ready.

Will customers know it's AI?

AI is designed to feel completely human — short messages, natural delays, Hinglish tone. Most customers never realize.

What languages are supported?

12+ Indian languages including Hindi, Hinglish, English, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia. Auto-detected.

Can I take over from AI?

Yes. One click in the dashboard pauses AI and lets you reply manually. Click again to resume.

Is my data safe?

Yes. All data is encrypted, stored securely, and never shared with third parties except required services (Meta, AI providers).

What if AI doesn't know an answer?

It asks you via the dashboard. You answer once — AI learns and remembers forever. This is the AI Learning Loop.
