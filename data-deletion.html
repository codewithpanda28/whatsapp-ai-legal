Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 18, 2026

How to Request Data Deletion

At WhatsApp AI Panel, you have the right to request deletion of your personal data at any time. Follow these steps:

Option 1: Email Request (Recommended)

  1. Send an email to support@yourdomain.com
  2. Subject: "Data Deletion Request"
  3. Include in body:
    • Your registered email address
    • Your business name
    • Your WhatsApp Business phone number
    • Confirmation that you want all your data deleted
  4. We will confirm receipt within 24 hours
  5. Data will be deleted within 7 business days

Option 2: In-App Deletion

  1. Log into your WhatsApp AI Panel account
  2. Go to Settings → Account
  3. Click "Delete My Account"
  4. Confirm deletion
  5. Data will be deleted within 7 business days

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Your account information (email, business name, phone)
  • All customer conversations and messages
  • Customer contact data stored in our system
  • All templates, knowledge base entries, and settings
  • Usage logs and analytics data

What Data Will Be Retained

We may retain the following for legal compliance:

  • Billing records (for tax/accounting purposes) — up to 7 years
  • Basic information required by Indian law (IT Act, GST)

Confirmation

After deletion, you will receive a confirmation email with a deletion verification code. Save this code for your records.

Third-Party Data

To delete data stored by Meta (WhatsApp), you must request deletion directly through:

  • Facebook Settings
  • WhatsApp Privacy Settings

Questions?

Contact: support@yourdomain.com
